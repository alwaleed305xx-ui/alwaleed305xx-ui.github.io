Rate my beast

Make it. Walk it. Get hearts.

A cute 3D creature game. Build your own plush beast, paint it however you like, walk it through five dreamy maps full of other players' beasts, and give hearts and laughs to the ones you love. Every heart you receive lifts your rank.

Free to play · optional in-app purchases · no ads · English and العربية · for everyone

A day in the park

Real screens from the game. Every beast is built from parts, colours and a paint layer, in one soft plush style.

How it works

Three minutes to make a beast, a lifetime to make it famous.

1

Make

Pick a body, eyes, mouth, ears, tail, legs and gear, push the sliders as far as you dare, choose any colour, then paint right on the fur with brushes and stamps.

2

Walk

Stroll Meadow Park, Sunny Beach, Snow Peak, Night City and Moon Base with a thumb joystick. The top beasts, the hottest today, the newest and everyone you follow are there with you.

3

Rate

Tap a beast, give it a heart for cute or a laugh for funny. Hearts you receive lift you through seven ranks, from Egg all the way to Legend.

Something new every day

A daily theme, a stage to judge on, boards to climb and friends to follow.

Daily challenge

A new theme each day: Pirate, Chef, Alien, Superhero, Wedding and many more. Enter with your beast, one entry a day. The top three win crowns and coins.

Judge and earn

Rate the entries on the stage: 5 coins per rating, up to 30 a day.

Leaderboards

Best, Funniest, Hot (the last 24 hours) and Friends. Your rank shows on your name tag.

People

Search a handle, follow players, and compare hearts side by side in Rivals.

Shop and packs

Rare parts, hats, gear, colours and maps for coins you earn by judging, hearts, daily gifts and prizes, or from a coin pack. The Kawaii, Party and Fantasy packs (frog hood, strawberry cap, unicorn horn, wizard hat, auras), the 30-day Beast Pass, VIP and Spotlight days are optional purchases. Nothing random is sold.

Safe and simple

No ads, and money never buys hearts or ranks. Map chat and direct messages are short, profanity-filtered and link-free; report any message or beast from its menu, block a player from their profile, or turn chat off in Settings. Objectionable content is removed and the accounts behind it are banned.
